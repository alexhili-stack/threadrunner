# Art and feedback

Dark navy facility, muted blue metal, warm white figure, teal scarf and exits, coral hazards and gold diamond fragments. Shape cues supplement colour: teeth, triangles, crosshatching, brackets, diamond. Grid and facility marks stay low contrast. UI uses Godot's bundled font with visible keyboard focus; minimum logical 640 x 360. Avoid flashing or effects obscuring landing surfaces.

PlayerVisuals independently depicts idle/run/jump/fall/wall slide. Screen shake is short and can be disabled; reduced effects suppresses it. Original sounds cover jump, wall jump, landing, death, fragment, clear, menu and optional respawn. Eight reusable SFX players cap overlap. Pause stops active effects; four original ambient chapter compositions crossfade through two reusable Music players. Missing resources safely skip playback. Audio sliders persist immediately, including mute. Subjective sound quality requires listening.

Chapter identity: teal signal circuitry, warm clockwork mechanisms, violet night windows, then a warmer Afterlight horizon. Decorative gear outlines are muted; moving lethal saws remain coral. Vents show neutral floor slits while safe, amber dots before firing and coral upward plumes while active. Gates turn amber shortly before closing. No flashing is used.

The courier stays visible during pause/completion; only actual death draws the short radial burst. Jump/landing dust, scarf motion, collection rings and exit pulses are bounded drawings, not accumulating nodes. Chapter music and controller deadzone are persistent settings.

Version 1.2 replaces the default typeface with licensed Barlow Condensed and Manrope and establishes the system in DESIGN.md. Quiet ink menus, chapter tabs, grouped settings, sharp platform edges and visible saw paths replace the old panel/grid presentation. The usable thread has square stitch terminals, round grapple sockets and a bounded rewind path. See docs/thread_tool.md and docs/visual_research.md.
