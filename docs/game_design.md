# Threadrunner 1.1

The user authorised expanding and polishing the original game on 6 November 2026. The campaign now has 40 rooms in four chapters: Boot Sequence, Clockwork, The Last Thread, and Afterlight. Rooms 1-30 remain single-screen 640x360 and use required vertical/switchback routes. Rooms 31-40 are scrolling 1280x360 courses with camera bounds and a fixed 640x360 logical viewport.

Movement remains running, variable jump, wall slide and wall jump with the shared tested MovementConfig. No per-room physics changes or new movement abilities. Hazards include floor spikes, ceiling teeth, moving saws, signalled gates and hot-air vents with a safe amber warning window. Same-attempt fragments are optional.

Five pilot rooms (1, 4, 8, 13, 30) established the revised route and presentation pattern. Geometry then expanded across the first 30 and into the ten larger courses. Floors no longer provide a direct hold-right bypass to exits. Automated normal/fragment routes and negative bypass attempts are evidence of executability, not human difficulty/fun assessment.

Each chapter has original procedural ambient music and a distinct restrained facility backdrop. Selection previews, names, PBs and fragments are visible; completion reports the previous-best difference. Settings include keyboard remapping, adjustable stick deadzone, music/SFX/master/mute, fullscreen, shake and reduced effects. Controller prompts adapt to the last meaningful input.

Save schema 2 preserves stable IDs, unlocks, completions, fragments and settings. Old-layout times are archived and cleared from current-layout PB competition. Human game-feel, physical controller/unplug tests and subjective listening remain separate from automated checks. No publishing or external services are part of this work.

## Version 1.2: the usable thread

The user requested bridge/relay stitching, grappling/swinging and trail rewind. All three now use one shared movement configuration and are taught in five additional Threadwork missions, giving 45 rooms. The original 40 gain authored thread terminals and sockets without changing their geometry. The rebuilt native interface uses an original thread identity, licensed display/body typography, chapter selection and grouped settings. See docs/thread_tool.md, DESIGN.md and the current acceptance report for behavior and evidence.
