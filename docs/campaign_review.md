## Version 1.2 — thread routes

The forty original room geometries and stable IDs are preserved, with authored bridge terminals and grapple sockets added. Their 3/3 ordinary and 3/3 fragment replays remain part of canonical QA. Five additional lessons use real six-input fixtures: move, jump edge, jump hold, stitch edge, grapple edge and rewind hold. No fixture teleports the courier, disables hazards or grants collectibles.
