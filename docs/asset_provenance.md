# Asset provenance

All room geometry, courier/scarf vector drawing, facility background, icon, HUD, menus and effects were authored for this project. They do not copy third-party game assets. Sound effects are original 22,050 Hz mono PCM synthesis from tools/create_content.py, with a fixed random seed for noise envelopes. Four original 24-second ambient compositions were generated by tools/create_ambience.py from sine harmonics and chord envelopes. No outside recordings, samples or external music were used.

Godot 4.7.2 and its bundled font/runtime components retain their notices in third_party/godot/NOTICES.txt, generated directly from Engine.get_license_text(), get_copyright_info() and get_license_info(). GdUnit4 is MIT; its licence is in addons/gdUnit4/LICENSE and third_party/gdunit4-LICENSE.txt. gdtoolkit is MIT; its licence is in third_party/gdtoolkit/LICENSE. Development add-ons and tests are excluded from the shipped PCK. The portable distribution includes third_party notices alongside the executable.

No generated media service or outside asset pack was required. Working-title availability, branding and final commercial licence review are human release tasks. No legal clearance claim is made by this file.
