# Asset provenance

All room geometry, courier/scarf vector drawing, icon, HUD, menus and native effects were authored for this project. They do not copy third-party game assets. Sound effects are original 22,050 Hz mono PCM synthesis from tools/create_content.py, with a fixed random seed for noise envelopes. Four original 24-second ambient compositions were generated by tools/create_ambience.py from sine harmonics and chord envelopes. No outside recordings, samples or external music were used.

Godot 4.7.2 and its bundled font/runtime components retain their notices in third_party/godot/NOTICES.txt, generated directly from Engine.get_license_text(), get_copyright_info() and get_license_info(). GdUnit4 is MIT; its licence is in addons/gdUnit4/LICENSE and third_party/gdunit4-LICENSE.txt. gdtoolkit is MIT; its licence is in third_party/gdtoolkit/LICENSE. Development add-ons and tests are excluded from the shipped PCK. The portable distribution includes third_party notices alongside the executable.

Earlier vector releases used no generated media service. The Electric Foundry revision adds the generated presentation assets recorded below; no outside game asset pack is used. Working-title availability, branding and final commercial licence review are human release tasks. No legal clearance claim is made by this file.

## Version 1.2 typography

Barlow Condensed SemiBold and Manrope variable font were downloaded from the official Google Fonts repository on 6 November 2026. Their SIL Open Font License 1.1 notices ship in third_party/fonts/Barlow-OFL.txt and Manrope-OFL.txt. Fonts are bundled project assets, not system installations.

- BarlowCondensed-SemiBold.ttf: https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/barlowcondensed/BarlowCondensed-SemiBold.ttf; SHA-256 `7b619d14bc2327509a9ef32b0890f709626f7ecc9ff61191c2a4314c5499d2d9`.
- Manrope.ttf: https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/manrope/Manrope%5Bwght%5D.ttf; SHA-256 `3ae11c49db0455a3cc33e37d380f20fdb8c7f8b41dc07625c177e3d87a9d6ae6`.

The slider knot, title courier, flowing scarf, woven exit, tool terminals, thread drawings and revised platform/hazard rendering are original native vector assets/code. Reference-game images were inspected in the browser and are not shipped.

## Version 1.4 Electric Foundry

The selected concept is preserved in docs/design/electric_foundry_reference.png. Three production raster assets were generated with the built-in Image Gen tool for this project: assets/art/foundry/background.png (1672 × 941), platform_surface.png (1254 × 1254), and electric_haze.png (1774 × 887, alpha). Their exact generation prompts, dimensions and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in [foundry_asset_provenance.json](foundry_asset_provenance.json). The background, repeated platform material and tinted haze are presentation layers; collision geometry is authored separately.

The pause icon is the Godot 4.7.2 Pause.svg from [the official pinned source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/godotengine/godot/4.7.2-stable/editor/icons/Pause.svg), used under the MIT notice included in third_party/godot/NOTICES.txt. No assets from reference platformer games are distributed.
