The reviewer inspected seventeen actual Godot captures covering 640 × 360 and 1280 × 720 menus, settings, gameplay and tool states. All captures were valid. No movement or hazard evaluation changed.

The reviewer re-read all seven requested gameplay/tool recaptures, scored the rail fix **resolved**, reported no material regressions from the batch and returned **disposition: ship**. This verdict covers that scored fix; it does not claim a human playtest or physical-controller assessment.

The documenter inspected source tokens/components and two final actual captures, updated only DESIGN.md and .impeccable/design.json, and validated their schema, references and narrative parity.
