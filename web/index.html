How to play

A D or arrows to move. Space to jump. R to retry. Esc to pause.

E stitches a bridge beside square terminals. F grabs a round anchor; steer to swing and jump to release. Hold Shift to rewind along your laid thread.

Controller: stick / D-pad, A jump, X stitch, B grapple, LB rewind, Y retry, Start pause. Keyboard bindings can be changed in Settings.

Progress saves in this browser. Gold fragments count when you finish the same attempt.
